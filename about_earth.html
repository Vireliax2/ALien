🌍 Earth's Formation

Earth formed approximately 4.54 billion years ago through accretion from the solar nebula. Volcanic outgassing created the primordial atmosphere, and water vapor condensed into oceans. The Moon formed soon afterward, possibly as the result of a Mars-sized body (Theia) colliding with Earth.

🌎 Planetary Characteristics

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known astronomical object to harbor life. About 29% of Earth's surface is land consisting of continents and islands, while the remaining 71% is covered by water, mostly by oceans but also lakes, rivers and other fresh water.

🌏 Earth's Structure

Earth's interior is divided into layers: the solid inner core, liquid outer core, viscous mantle, and solid crust. The crust and upper mantle form lithospheric plates that move relative to each other, causing continental drift, earthquakes, and volcanic activity.

🌍 Climate and Weather

Earth's climate varies from tropical at the equator to polar at the poles. Weather systems are driven by solar energy, Earth's rotation, and atmospheric circulation. The greenhouse effect maintains Earth's average temperature at about 15°C (59°F), making it habitable.

🌎 Life on Earth

Earth is the only known planet with life. About 8.7 million species exist, though only about 1.9 million have been identified. Life first appeared about 3.8 billion years ago and has evolved through natural selection into the incredible biodiversity we see today.

🌏 Human Civilization

Modern humans emerged about 300,000 years ago in Africa. Agriculture began about 12,000 years ago, leading to settled societies. Today, Earth is home to nearly 8 billion people in about 200 nations, with diverse cultures, languages, and political systems.

🌍 Future of Earth

Earth's future depends on both natural processes and human activity. In about 1 billion years, the Sun's increasing luminosity will make Earth too hot for liquid water. Before then, human-caused climate change and environmental degradation pose significant challenges to civilization.
