Famous Landmarks Across the World

Trevi Fountain — Rome, Italy

Rome's a city with no shortage of icons, but the Trevi Fountain is among the most timeless. With roots dating back to Roman engineering, this masterpiece represents their ability to tame water and decorate cities with awe-inspiring beauty.

Eiffel Tower — Paris, France

The Eiffel Tower, or "La Dame de Fer," was built for the 1889 World Fair and has since become France's most recognizable icon. Once hated, now loved, it stands as a steel skeleton with a beating cultural heart.

Great Wall of China — China

Stretching over 13,000 miles, the Great Wall is less a wall and more a colossal flex of ancient Chinese determination. Dynasties built it to defend, control, and define borders—a legacy now preserved as a UNESCO marvel.

Chichen Itza — Yucatan, Mexico

Chichen Itza stands as the crown jewel of Mayan genius. The Kukulkan Pyramid isn't just pretty—it's aligned with the equinoxes like some ancient architectural horoscope. Math, astronomy, and spiritual vibes? Mayans nailed it.

Taj Mahal — Agra, India

Built by Emperor Shah Jahan for his queen, the Taj Mahal is love set in white marble. Twenty-two years, 20,000 workers, and endless artistry went into creating this flawless mausoleum that continues to hypnotize visitors worldwide.
