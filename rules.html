Earth Entry Protocols

I. Hostile Action Prohibition

Use of force against humans is strictly prohibited. Any individual or group causing harm, directly or indirectly, will be neutralized or detained without negotiation.

II. Controlled Mobility

Unsupervised movement across Earth is forbidden. All interstellar visitors must be accompanied by a certified human representative or assigned escort unless granted independent clearance.

III. Mental and Neural Interference Ban

Psychic, telepathic, or neurological manipulation of any kind is banned. Violators will be restrained under maximum containment protocols.

IV. Environmental Protection Clause

No alterations to Earth’s environment or ecosystems are permitted. Terraforming, atmospheric engineering, and biological tampering are treated as acts of aggression.

V. Non-Consensual Contact Ban

Abductions, invasive examinations, or sampling of human or animal life without explicit consent are illegal. Immediate retaliation may follow.

VI. Technology Regulation

Weapons, advanced machinery, or autonomous systems must be declared and stored under international supervision. Deployment without clearance is grounds for containment.

VII. Communications Interference Restriction

Tampering with or disrupting Earth-based communication networks is classified as hostile activity.

VIII. Cultural and Territorial Respect

All entities must respect human cultures, borders, and international laws. Disrespect or desecration may lead to diplomatic revocation or expulsion.

IX. Genetic Manipulation Prohibition

Creation of hybrids or biological experiments involving humans or Earth species is strictly forbidden.

X. Mandatory Registration and Identification

All extraterrestrial visitors must submit biological and technological data for official documentation prior to integration.

XI. Threat Assessment Clause

Entities deemed a potential threat to humanity’s safety, stability, or sovereignty will be captured and detained indefinitely in secured facilities.

XII. Interdimensional and Extra-Temporal Limitations

Time manipulation, dimensional interference, and reality alteration are classified as existential threats and are outlawed.

XIII. Biological Safety Compliance

All visitors must undergo biological quarantine and clearance to ensure no pathogens, parasites, or biological contaminants are introduced.

XIV. Non-Disclosure Enforcement

Extraterrestrials must not expose their presence to the global public without government sanction. Violations will result in immediate protocol lockdown.
